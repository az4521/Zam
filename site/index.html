A pretty good Matrix client

Zam is a free, open source Matrix client for the web, desktop, and Android, designed to be familiar to users of Discord.

Open the web app Download

Works with any Matrix homeserver. Sign in with the account you already have.

What's in it

A few of the things Zam does. The full list is in the README.

Voice and video calls

Group calls over MatrixRTC with screen sharing, per-person volume and noise suppression. On Android they behave like phone calls and ring over the lock screen.

Needs a LiveKit server

End-to-end encryption

Encrypted rooms and DMs, emoji and QR verification, cross-signing and key backup. Search works in encrypted rooms too, from an index kept on your device.

Threads and rich messages

Threads, replies, edits, reactions, polls, voice messages, live location, custom emoji and stickers, and Discord-style markdown with spoilers.

Spaces that you arrange

Drag rooms into coloured folders, collapse sub-spaces into categories, and have the order sync across your devices.

Notifications that work

Full push rules, with encrypted messages decrypted for the notification even when the app is closed. Android supports Firebase or UnifiedPush.

Make it yours

Light, dark and true-black themes, your own colour presets, custom CSS, and plugins you install from GitHub.

Rich presence

On desktop, show the game or music you're into on your profile and see what others are up to, like on Discord.

Modern sign-in

Password, SSO, OAuth 2.0 / OIDC, or scan a QR code from a device that's already signed in. Multiple accounts supported.

Open source

GPL-3.0 licensed and built in the open. Translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese and Assyrian Neo-Aramaic.
